\chapter*{摘\quad 要}\chaptermark{摘\quad 要}% 摘要标题
\setcounter{page}{1}% 开始页码
\pagenumbering{Roman}% 页码符号

动态目标抓取要求机械臂在目标持续平移和旋转时完成接近、相对位姿对齐、夹爪闭合、保持和搬运。与静态抓取或单步轨迹跟踪相比，该任务同时涉及运动目标的相对表示、短时历史利用和闭环时机判断。本文以动态立方体抓取为研究对象，构建目标中心动作块模仿学习框架，并在统一数据、统一动作接口和统一闭环执行协议下比较多类学习策略。研究重点不在于提出单一新模型，而在于分析哪些设计因素真正决定动态目标抓取的闭环成功率。

基于5000条成功专家示教和每个主要条件750回合闭环评估，本文得到三个主要发现。第一，目标中心表示是最稳定的性能来源：以ACT为例，使用目标中心表示时成功率为57.3\%--59.2\%，切换至世界坐标表示后降至8.8\%--27.2\%，表示带来的差距明显大于ACT与BC-RNN之间的架构差距。第二，随机姿态翻滚平移场景需要时间结构和动作块输出：单步BC几乎完全失败（0.1\%），而BC-RNN和ACT分别达到48.7\%和59.2\%，说明历史上下文和多步动作组织对于抓取面选择、姿态对齐和闭合时机判断是必要的。第三，模型容量或策略类别本身并不能保证成功：在本文低维动作空间和10步DDIM设置下，DP各容量版本仍处于1.3\%--2.8\%的低成功率区间，因此动态抓取性能更受表示和时间结构约束，而非简单由模型复杂度决定。

噪声实验进一步表明，状态估计策略需要与控制器结构匹配。专家FSM-MPC控制器在强噪声下完全失效（0.0\%），加入卡尔曼滤波后恢复至56.4\%，说明显式状态估计对基于规则和优化的控制器至关重要。相比之下，ACT和BC-RNN在卡尔曼滤波后的变化仅为$+2.5$和$-5.2$个百分点，表明学习策略已通过历史窗口和动作块结构获得一定隐式时间平滑能力。综合来看，在FSM-MPC专家示教框架内，动态目标抓取的有效学习控制主要取决于目标中心表示、时间动作块和状态估计方式的协同，而不是单纯更换或扩大策略架构。

\chapter*{Abstract}\chaptermark{Abstract}% 摘要标题

Dynamic object grasping requires a manipulator to approach, align with, close on, hold, and transport a target that is continuously moving. Compared with static grasping or one-step trajectory tracking, this task depends on relative motion representation, short-horizon temporal context, and closed-loop timing. This thesis studies dynamic cube grasping through a target-centric action-chunk imitation learning framework, where multiple learned policies are compared under the same data, action interface, and execution protocol. The goal is not to propose another isolated architecture, but to identify which design factors determine closed-loop success in moving-target manipulation.

Based on 5000 successful expert demonstrations and 750 closed-loop evaluation episodes per main condition, the experiments lead to three main findings. First, target-centric representation is the most stable source of performance: ACT reaches 57.3--59.2\% success with target-centric state, but drops to 8.8--27.2\% with world-frame state, a gap larger than the architectural difference between ACT and BC-RNN. Second, random-orientation tumbling translation requires temporal structure and action chunks: one-step BC almost completely fails (0.1\%), whereas BC-RNN and ACT reach 48.7\% and 59.2\%, showing that history and multi-step action organization are necessary for grasp-face selection, pose alignment, and closing timing. Third, model class or capacity alone does not ensure success: under the low-dimensional action space and 10-step DDIM setup used in this thesis, all DP variants remain in the 1.3--2.8\% success range, suggesting that representation and temporal structure are stronger constraints than model scale in this setting.

The noise experiments further show that state estimation should be matched to the controller structure. The expert FSM-MPC controller fails completely under strong perceptual noise (0.0\%), but recovers to 56.4\% with a Kalman filter, indicating that explicit state estimation is critical for rule- and optimization-based control. In contrast, ACT and BC-RNN change by only $+2.5$ and $-5.2$ percentage points with Kalman filtering, suggesting that learned temporal policies already obtain limited implicit smoothing through history windows and action chunks. Overall, within the FSM-MPC expert demonstration framework, effective learned control for dynamic grasping depends on the coordination of target-centric representation, temporal action chunks, and controller-specific state estimation, rather than on replacing or scaling the policy architecture alone.
